CLIENT CALL #3 — Internet Outage + Mobile Plan Upgrade + Billing Dispute
==========================================================================

SCENARIO: A frustrated customer calls their telecom provider about
ongoing connectivity problems. The call shifts through three topics.

Read this naturally, as if you're actually on the phone. Pause briefly
between sentences. Pause a bit longer between the [TOPIC SHIFT] markers.
Start with a slightly frustrated tone for the first part.

---

Hi, yes, I'd like to report an issue with my internet connection.
My name is Alessandro Rocca, and my customer number is eight zero
six one four three. I've been having problems with my home internet
for the past three days now. The connection keeps dropping every
twenty to thirty minutes, and when it is working, the speed is
terrible — I ran a speed test and I'm only getting about twelve
megabits per second, but I'm paying for the one-gigabit fiber plan.
I've already tried restarting the router multiple times, and I even
did a factory reset yesterday, but nothing changed. I work from home
as a software developer, and this is really affecting my ability to
do my job. I need someone to look into this urgently. My address is
Bahnhofstrasse forty-two, eight thousand and one Zurich. Could you
send a technician as soon as possible?

[TOPIC SHIFT — pause 2 seconds]

Okay, while we sort that out, I also wanted to ask about my mobile
plan. I'm currently on the basic plan with five gigabytes of data per
month, but honestly that's not enough anymore. I keep running out of
data by the third week. What options do you have for unlimited data
plans? And I travel to Italy and Germany pretty often for work, so
I'd need something that includes European roaming without extra
charges. Also, is it possible to keep my current phone number if I
switch plans? The number is zero seven nine three four five six seven
eight nine.

[TOPIC SHIFT — pause 2 seconds]

Actually, speaking of plans, I just looked at my last invoice and I
think there's a mistake. The bill from May shows a charge of
forty-five francs for something called "Premium Services," but I
never signed up for any premium services. I have no idea what that is.
The invoice number is INV dash two zero two six dash zero five dash
one seven eight nine. Could you check what that charge is for and
remove it if it's an error? Because I definitely didn't authorize it.
If there's been an overcharge, I'd like a credit applied to my next
bill. Thank you.
