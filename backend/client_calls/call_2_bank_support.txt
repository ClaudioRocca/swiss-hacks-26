CLIENT CALL #2 — Suspicious Transaction + Mortgage Inquiry + Standing Order
=============================================================================

SCENARIO: A customer calls their bank after noticing a strange charge
on their account. The call naturally evolves through three topics.

Read this naturally, as if you're actually on the phone. Pause briefly
between sentences. Pause a bit longer between the [TOPIC SHIFT] markers.

---

Hello, yes, hi. I'm calling because I just noticed a charge on my
current account that I don't recognize at all. My name is Alessandro
Rocca, account number four four dash seven eight one three six zero.
The transaction was on June eighteenth, for two hundred and forty-nine
Swiss francs, and it says it's from something called "DigiStore
Twenty-Four" based in Germany. I've never heard of this company, and
I definitely didn't authorize any purchase for that amount. I'm
worried someone might have gotten hold of my card details. The card
ends in eight three four seven. Could you please look into this and
block the card if necessary? I really don't want any more charges
going through.

[TOPIC SHIFT — pause 2 seconds]

Alright, thank you for taking care of that. Now, on a completely
different topic — my partner and I are looking to buy an apartment
here in Zurich, and I wanted to get some information about your
mortgage offers. We're looking at a property in Oerlikon that's
listed at around one point two million francs, and we have about
three hundred thousand saved for the down payment. What kind of
interest rate could we get on a ten-year fixed mortgage? And what
documents would we need to bring in to start the application process?
We're both employed, if that helps — I work in software engineering
and she's a doctor at the Universitätsspital.

[TOPIC SHIFT — pause 2 seconds]

Perfect, I'll gather those documents. Oh, actually, one last thing.
I need to set up a monthly standing order from my current account to
my landlord. His name is Thomas Keller, and his IBAN is CH nine three
zero zero seven six seven zero one two three four five six seven eight
nine zero. The amount should be two thousand one hundred francs on the
first of every month, starting July first. Can you set that up for me
right now? Thank you, you've been very helpful.
