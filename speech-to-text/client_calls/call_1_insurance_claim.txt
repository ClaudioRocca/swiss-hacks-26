CLIENT CALL #1 — Insurance Claim + Policy Upgrade + Address Change
===================================================================

SCENARIO: A customer calls their car insurance company after a minor
accident. The call naturally shifts through three distinct topics.

Read this naturally, as if you're actually on the phone. Pause briefly
between sentences. Pause a bit longer between the [TOPIC SHIFT] markers.

---

Hi, good afternoon. My name is Alessandro Rocca, and I'm calling about
a car accident I was involved in earlier today. My policy number is
CH dash seven four two nine one. So what happened is, I was driving on
the A2 motorway near Lugano, and a car in front of me braked suddenly.
I couldn't stop in time and I rear-ended them at low speed, maybe
twenty or thirty kilometers per hour. Nobody was hurt, thankfully, but
my front bumper is pretty damaged, and the other driver's rear light is
cracked. I already took photos of both vehicles and I have the other
driver's details. His name is Marco Bernasconi, and his plate number is
TI three five seven eight nine two. I'd like to file a claim and
understand what the next steps are.

[TOPIC SHIFT — pause 2 seconds]

Actually, while I have you on the line, I also wanted to ask about
upgrading my coverage. Right now I have third-party liability only, but
after what happened today, I'm thinking I should probably get
comprehensive coverage — you know, full kasko. Could you tell me how
much extra that would cost per month? And does it cover things like
hail damage and theft as well? I drive a 2022 Volkswagen Golf, if that
matters for the quote.

[TOPIC SHIFT — pause 2 seconds]

Oh, one more thing before we finish. I moved apartments last month and
I need to update my billing address. The new address is Via Nassa
fifteen, sixty-nine hundred Lugano. Can you update that in the system?
And could you also send the next invoice to my new email, which is
a dot rocca at gmail dot com? That would be great. Thank you so much
for your help.
